    <section class="be-card" style="margin-bottom: 40px;">  
      <h2>What is a Value-Focused Buyer?</h2>  
      <p>  
        A Value-Focused Buyer is someone looking to purchase a property at a discount. They understand that the property will be in a <strong>less-than-market-ready state</strong>—perhaps outdated, damaged, or simply neglected.  
      </p>  
      <p style="margin-bottom: 0;">  
        Unlike the average buyer looking for "turnkey," the Value-Focused Buyer sees the imperfection as an opportunity. By using <strong>sweat equity</strong> (their own labor or management) to improve the condition, they force the value of the home up, rather than waiting years for the market to do it for them.  
      </p>  
    </section>  
  
    <!-- THE TWO MAJOR BENEFITS -->

          Buying below market value offers two distinct financial advantages over the traditional "retail" purchase.  
        </p>  
      </div>

    <!-- THE RISK (BPO) -->  
    <section class="be-card" style="margin-bottom: 40px; background: #eff6ff; border-color: #dbeafe;">  
      <h2 style="margin-top: 0;">The Risk: Is the Discount Real?</h2>  
      <p>  
        The danger is assuming that a low price automatically equals a good deal. Sometimes, a cheap house is expensive because the repairs exceed the potential value.  
      </p>  
      <p>  
        To execute this strategy successfully, you must verify the numbers before you buy. A <span class="be-highlight">Broker Price Opinion (BPO)</span> helps you confirm:  
      </p>  
      <ul style="margin: 0; padding-left: 20px; color: #475569;">  
        <li>The true "As-Is" market value (are you actually getting a discount?).</li>  
        <li>The "After Repair Value" (ARV) (is the ceiling high enough?).</li>  
        <li>The feasibility of your budget vs. the reality of the repairs.</li>  
      </ul>  
    </section>  
  
    <!-- FHA 203(k) SECTION -->  
    <section class="be-card" style="margin-bottom: 40px; border-left: 5px solid #1e3a8a;">  
      <h2 style="margin-top: 0;">The Secret Weapon: FHA 203(k)</h2>  
      <p>  
        The FHA 203(k) loan is the value-focused buyer's most powerful tool. It allows you to purchase a property with little money down <strong>and include the renovations in your mortgage</strong>. You make one monthly payment for both the house and the upgrades.  
      </p>  
        
      <div class="be-grid-2" style="margin-top: 30px;">

            <li>Current "As-Is" Value.</li>

            <li>Anticipated "After-Repair Value" (ARV).</li>
